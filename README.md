### 🧭 What I Build

I design and ship end-to-end software for Indian SMBs — from offline-first mobile apps to multi-tenant SaaS platforms. I work across four main areas:

- **Health Tech** — offline-first AI-augmented health records apps with wearable integration
- **Business SaaS** — pharmacy stock management, spinning mill maintenance, dunning/accounts receivable systems
- **Agri & Commerce** — e-commerce platforms for farms, local city directories
- **Developer Tools & Games** — hardware monitoring apps, WhatsApp automation, Unity sci-fi runners

I'm a **Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH)**, and I bring a security-first mindset into every backend I design.

### 🧱 My Coding Doctrine

Every project I build — solo or at work — follows a personal 12-principle doctrine: **Clean Architecture, mandatory audit fields, soft-delete only, explicit-over-implicit design, layered responsibility (Controller → Service → Repository), and typed contracts everywhere.**

📄 Full writeup: [`coding-doctrine`](https://github.com/gogulgotu/coding-doctrine) *(link once repo is created)*
